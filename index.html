How we work together

Twelve weeks, one change at a time.

A diet chart alone rarely changes anything. The weekly conversation does. This is the usual shape of a programme.

  1. Day 0

    First conversation

    A short call about your goal, your history and what you've already tried.

  2. Days 1–3

    Food diary and reports

    You log three ordinary days and share your recent blood reports and medicines.

  3. Week 1

    Full assessment

    Routine, work hours, likes, dislikes, budget, who cooks, and what's in the kitchen.

  4. Week 1

    Your written plan

    Meal by meal, in your timings, with swaps for eating out, travel and festivals.

  5. Weeks 2–11

    Weekly check-ins

    We review what worked, fix what didn't and adjust portions as your body responds.

  6. Week 12

    Review and handover

    Repeat labs, compare the numbers, and a maintenance plan you can run alone.

    Questions

    Before you book

    Will I have to give up rice, roti or sweets?

    No. We adjust how much, how often and what goes beside them. Foods only come off the plate for a medical reason, and I'll tell you the reason.

    I live outside India. Can you still help?

    Yes. Consults are by video, your reports are shared online, and the plan uses ingredients you can buy where you live.

    Should I stop my medicines once I start the diet?

    No. Never change a medicine without your doctor. Diet works alongside treatment, and if your numbers improve, your doctor decides what to reduce.

    How quickly will I see results?

    Energy and digestion often improve in the first weeks. For weight, about half a kilo to one kilo a week is a safe pace. I don't promise a number, because bodies differ.

    What should I keep ready for the first consult?

    Recent blood reports, a list of your medicines and supplements, and a simple diary of everything you ate for three ordinary days.

    I'm vegetarian, vegan or Jain. Is that a problem?

    Not at all. Plans are built inside your food rules, with attention to protein, iron and B12 where they tend to run low.
